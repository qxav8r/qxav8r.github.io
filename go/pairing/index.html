How trip pay works

From the IBT contract, credit hours (CBA 5.C.2.a). Plain-language summary; the contract governs.

  • Each calendar day (00:00–23:59 LBase) earns the greater of the block flown in that day, including deadheads, or the 4:12 Minimum Daily Guarantee.
  • The trip pays the greatest of: all its calendar days added up (MDCR), 25% of TAFB, or 50% of duty. That winner is Trip Pay.
  • CBLK (total block) is shown for reference. It is not one of the contract's three methods and cannot beat MDCR.
  • The 4:12 daily minimum does not apply to functional flights, training, company business, or trips split by the pilot (trades or drops). On those, this result can run high.
  • Premium pay (for example time and a half) is applied after the trip value is set, and is not shown here.
  • REG bucket is the daily credit RainMaker should show. If RainMaker differs, look closer at that day.

Irregular operations

If a trip was affected by IRROPS (cancelled, added or rerouted legs, or a delay that moved a leg to another day) and was not flown as scheduled, this result reflects only the trip as flown. A more complete analysis is needed.

If you have the original trip, run it too and compare: that shows which version pays more. Tip: save the pairing's web archive when the trip is awarded, before anything changes, so you have the original to run later.

Definitions

LBase: the pilot's base time zone. Block, TAFB, Duty and MDCR are all worked in LBase.

Calendar day: 00:00 to 23:59 in LBase.

CBLK: sum of every flight's MBK. Duty: sum of all duty periods × 0.50. TAFB: first duty start to last duty end × 0.25. MDCR: sum of each calendar day's block, at least 4:12 a day.

Trip Pay: the highest of the four rigs.

REG bucket: the daily credit RainMaker should show. TAFB trips are averaged across the trip's days, with any rounding remainder on the last day.

Flights crossing midnight: each calendar day compares the scheduled block that falls in that day with the actual block that falls in that day, and takes the larger. A leg delayed past midnight can therefore count a little more across the two days than its own MBK.

Legs flown on a different day than scheduled (delay or IRROPS): the scheduled block stays on the scheduled day and the actual block goes on the day it was flown; each day still gets at least 4:12.

TAFB runs from the first duty's report to the last duty's release, using eCrew's own duty dates, so a release after midnight is handled.

! / ? on a flight number: the leg was split or moved because it crosses LBase midnight. ----: no scheduled or actual time on that piece.

Estimated (E) times are used as actuals; the result is provisional until actuals post.
