ATA (In): every leg that has departed gets an In box you can edit. Alaska only reports the On (touchdown) time, not block-in, so once landed the box starts with Alaska's On time; change it to your block-in time, e.g. 16:12 or 1612. Before landing it's empty: type your In once you're at the gate, or leave it empty to send just the Out time.

STD/STA: off by default so your IBT contract scheduled times in LogTen stay put. Alaska's are the public ORA times.
