Leave STD/STA off: eCrew has your IBT contract times, and Flighty shows the airline's public ORA times. Why?

United, Delta, SkyWest and other airlines, plus Alaska flights outside 2000–2999 (Alaska mainline), go to LogTen as deadheads. Horizon flights and Alaska 2000–2999 are always sent as your own flights.
