Floggy

eCrew to logbook. No retyping.

Mac app · v2.5 · 2.6 coming this week

What it does

Floggy is a free Mac app, built by a line pilot, that turns the files you save from eCrew into clean flight records.

  • Reads the eCrew schedule, Pilot Logbook report and pairing pages, plus Flighty exports.
  • Imports flights and crew into LogTen Pro.
  • Puts trips, duty days or flights on Apple Calendar or any calendar that takes an .ics file.
  • Exports CSV, and works out rig (TAFB, duty, CBLK, MDCR) for a pairing.
  • Converts times correctly across every station's time zone and daylight saving time.

On an iPad, or just want a quick LogTen import? Try Floggy-Go in your browser instead: no install, and your files stay on your device.
